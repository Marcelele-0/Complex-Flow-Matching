\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

Recent advances in \textbf{Stochastic Dynamics and Geometry} have demonstrated that tailoring generative models to the intrinsic topology of data significantly improves physical validity. \citet{debortoli2022riemannian} established the foundations for Riemannian Score-Based Generative Modeling by solving stochastic differential equations (SDEs) on compact manifolds, while \citet{debortoli2021diffusion} introduced Diffusion Schr{\"o}dinger Bridges to generalize score-based modeling with theoretically grounded Optimal Transport paths. More recently, Flow Matching \citep{lipman2023flow} provided a simulation-free approach for continuous normalizing flows, which \citet{chen2024flow} extended to general geometries, enabling scalable training on Riemannian manifolds. Our approach connects these frameworks to phase topology by leveraging closed-form Optimal Transport on the circle \citep{delon2010fast}, establishing a structure-preserving dynamical system specifically tailored for complex-valued inverse problems.
