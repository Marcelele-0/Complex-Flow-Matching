\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
By formulating probability paths on $\mathbb{R}^+ \times S^1$, Cylindrical Flow Matching natively resolves phase discontinuities and magnitude attenuation, providing a physically consistent generative baseline for complex-valued MRI. Future work will extend this framework to multi-coil acceleration benchmarks and frequency-domain counterfactual Explainable AI (XAI) on raw $k$-space \citep{bedel2024dreamr, min2025instructx2x}, bypassing spatial hallucination issues. Finally, extending this cylindrical manifold formulation to continuous Schr{\"o}dinger Bridges will enable data-driven priors for downstream tasks such as complex medical image segmentation.
