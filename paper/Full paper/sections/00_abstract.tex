Generative modeling of complex-valued Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) data remains a significant challenge due to the distinct topological properties of magnitude and phase, where the latter resides on a circular manifold $S^1$. Standard Euclidean models often fail to capture these non-linearities, resulting in phase artifacts and loss of high-frequency anatomical details. In this paper, we propose a Cylindrical Flow Matching framework tailored for complex MRI synthesis. We formulate the probability path on a cylindrical manifold and introduce a Decoupled Cylindrical Loss. Furthermore, we introduce a High-Frequency k-space Boost Loss leveraging 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to explicitly penalize high-frequency attenuation, ensuring sharp reconstruction of cartilage and bone boundaries. Evaluated on the SKM-TEA dataset, our slice-by-slice 2D generative approach demonstrates physically coherent complex MRI synthesis, bridging the gap between generative modeling and complex-valued clinical data.
