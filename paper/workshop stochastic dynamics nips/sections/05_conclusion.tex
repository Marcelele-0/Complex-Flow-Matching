\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
By formulating probability paths on $\mathbb{R}^+ \times S^1$, Cylindrical Flow Matching natively resolves phase discontinuities and magnitude attenuation, providing a physically consistent generative baseline for complex-valued MRI. Future work will extend this framework to multi-coil acceleration benchmarks and quantitative phase mapping. Finally, extending this cylindrical manifold formulation to continuous Schr{\"o}dinger Bridges will enable data-driven priors for downstream complex inverse problems and diagnostic tasks.
